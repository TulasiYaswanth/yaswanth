Delivered result

Invoice processing, ~20,000 invoices a month.

Vendor invoices arrived by email and portal, were manually keyed, matched and routed for approval by a four-person team. Automation now handles capture, classification, validation and routing — with humans handling only exceptions.

Before → after

Four full-time employees, reduced to one

4 → 1 FTEs on invoice processing

What changed: every invoice is captured on arrival, classified, validated against the vendor and purchase record, and routed to the correct approver. Missing information triggers a request automatically instead of sitting in an inbox.

What improved: processing speed, fewer manual keying errors, and a single exception queue instead of four people performing the same checks. The same team now absorbs volume that previously required hiring.

Modeled engagements

What we would target from day one.

Representative scenarios built from industry benchmarks and our delivery playbooks — not client testimonials. The Profit Leak Assessment exists to replace these numbers with yours.

Modeled · Home services

Kitchen remodeler — $3M/yr, 12 employees

+$840K annual revenue from lifting close rate 20% → 24% — roughly one extra job per month

The leak: ~25 estimates a month at a $70K average job, closing 20%. After the estimate goes out, follow-up depends on whoever remembers — most prospects hear nothing while competitors respond faster.

The fix: five-minute speed-to-lead auto-response, plus an automated estimate follow-up sequence at day 2, 5 and 10 — financing options, portfolio and a booking link included.

Modeled · Healthcare

Medical billing firm — 30 employees, ~25K claims/mo

+$200–300K additional annual collections, plus ~2 FTEs freed to onboard new accounts without hiring

The leak: 11% of claims deny on first pass. Staff burn ~3 FTEs checking eligibility and claim status across payer portals, and 30% of denials are never reworked because chasing them manually isn't worth it.

The fix: automated eligibility verification before submission, denial triage that routes reworkable claims by dollar value, and auto-generated client reports.

Modeled · Logistics

Freight brokerage — 15 employees, ~450 loads/mo

+$210K gross margin from ~50 extra loads/mo at $350 average; two reps back on the phones, quotes out in under 10 minutes

The leak: every load needs 3–4 check calls for track-and-trace — about 140 hours a month, effectively two reps doing status updates instead of selling. Quotes take 45+ minutes, and loads go to whoever answers first.

The fix: automated carrier check-ins by text and email that write status back to the TMS and flag exceptions only, plus auto-drafted responses to inbound rate requests.

Modeled engagements are representative scenarios built from industry benchmarks and our delivery playbooks — not client testimonials. Your assessment replaces these numbers with your own baseline.
