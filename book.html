FAQ

Questions worth asking before a call.

If yours isn't here, ask it on the assessment — that's what the 30 minutes are for.

Is this just another software subscription?

No. Flowza installs a system — workflows, automations, dashboards, AI agents and trained people — around your operation. Where software is needed we integrate the best fit rather than reselling a single platform.

How can you guarantee $200,000 in 120 days?

Because we don't start with random tools. We start by identifying where your business is leaking profit, then build around the highest-impact opportunities and baseline the numbers first. If the improvement isn't reached within 120 days, you get a full refund.

What if we're not sure what should be automated?

That's the normal starting point. The assessment exists to turn "everything feels manual" into a prioritised list with numbers attached, ordered by profit impact rather than by what's easiest to build.

Will AI make decisions without us?

Routine decisions can run automatically. Anything unusual, sensitive or financially material routes to a person for approval, denial or correction. Automation without losing control of the business.

How much of our team's time will this take?

Expect a handful of working sessions during discovery and testing, plus training near handover. The system is designed to reduce your team's workload, not add a second job managing it.

What happens after the 120 days?

You keep the documented system, the dashboards and the trained team. Ongoing support and optimisation are optional — the goal is that you're not dependent on us to operate your own business.
