The thesis

Your employees should not be the glue holding your systems together. They should not spend their best hours copying, pasting, chasing, reminding, searching, re-entering and reporting.

Growing service businesses rarely fail because of effort. They stall because too much of the work is robotic: the same follow-up, the same data entry, the same document chase, the same status question. That work is paid for every month, and it produces no margin.

Flowza installs the operating system your business should have already had — one connected layer for leads, communication, documents, estimates, invoices, payments, approvals and reporting.

How we work

Four principles that keep this honest.

These are the rules we hold ourselves to on every engagement — they are also the reason we can offer a profit guarantee.

Diagnose before you prescribe

No tool gets recommended until we have quantified the leak it is supposed to close. If the number isn't there, it doesn't make the roadmap.

Principle 01

Humans keep judgement

AI agents handle routine decisions. Anything unusual, sensitive or expensive routes to a person for approval, denial or correction.

Principle 02

Document everything

Process maps, runbooks and training are part of the deliverable. You should be able to operate and extend the system without us.

Principle 03

Report in dollars

Hours saved, response times, days-to-invoice, recovered opportunities. If we can't measure it, we don't claim it.

Principle 04
